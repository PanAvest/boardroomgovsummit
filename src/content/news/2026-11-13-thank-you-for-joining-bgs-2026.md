Dear Distinguished Delegates,

On behalf of the Boardroom Governance Summit (BGS) Africa, thank you for joining us yesterday at the Labadi Beach Hotel, Accra, and online, for BGS 2026 under the theme “Board Committees: From Oversight to Impact.”

You came from across Ghana and the African continent. Many of you rearranged demanding schedules, travelled long distances and gave a full day to a conversation that rarely makes headlines but shapes almost everything: how decisions are made in the rooms where the future of our institutions is decided.

Allow me to leave you with a question to carry back to your boardrooms. If your board committees were removed tomorrow, would anyone notice the difference? If the honest answer is “not much,” then the work of yesterday has only just begun. The true test of a committee is not whether it meets, but whether it changes outcomes. It is not whether it reviews reports, but whether it asks the question no one else in the room is willing to ask.

Africa does not lack talent, resources or ambition. What we must continue to build is the discipline to govern them well. Every audit question asked courageously, every risk confronted early, and every decision taken with the next generation in mind is a quiet act of nation-building. Some of the most consequential leadership on our continent will never be seen on a stage. It will happen around a boardroom table, in the patient, principled work of men and women like you.

As you return to your organisations, we hope you take with you more than notes and a book. We hope you take a renewed conviction that good governance is not a burden of compliance but a privilege of stewardship.

Thank you for your presence, your insights and your candour. We look forward to welcoming you, and the colleagues you will inspire to join you, at BGS 2027.

With deep appreciation and warm regards,

Ing. Prof. Douglas Boateng
Founder and Convenor
Boardroom Governance Summit (BGS) Africa
